The road to a Massachusetts license

  1. Learner's permit: pass the RMV knowledge test.
  2. Practice: supervised driving and lessons with an instructor.
  3. Road test: schedule with the RMV and drive with an examiner.
  4. License: your Class D driver's license.
Requirements vary by age. Drivers under 18 have extra driver's education rules. Check the current rules at mass.gov/rmv.

FAQ

Common questions

What should I bring to my lesson?

Your learner's permit, comfortable shoes and glasses or contacts if you use them. If you're preparing for the road test, bring the documents the RMV asks for.

How long does it take to be ready for the road test?

It depends on your experience. Some students need a few lessons, others more. After your first lesson, your instructor will tell you honestly how many lessons you'll likely need.

Can I use the school car for my road test?

Yes. We offer a car for test day, usually with a short warm-up lesson before your appointment. Call to check availability for your test date.

What documents does the RMV ask for?

Usually proof of identity, proof of Massachusetts residency and your Social Security number (or proof you're not eligible). Massachusetts also accepts certain foreign documents. The list changes, so always check the official checklist at mass.gov/rmv before your appointment.

Do you teach in Portuguese and Spanish?

Yes. Tell us your preferred language when you call or book, and we'll match you with the right instructor.
